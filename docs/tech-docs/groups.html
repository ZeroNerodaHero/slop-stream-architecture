groups

a login join a group. its chat lines wear the badge. a group is also a server, with channels.

the badge

a login
groupbadge · colour
chat linename + badge

a small box in the group's colour. a word in capitals. next to your name in chat, the way discord tags a bot.

one group is built in: bot. the fake streams' chatters wear it. the robot button in the chat hides their lines. nobody can make it or join it.

make one

any account can. you type a title. a-z 0-9 _ -, 1 to 32. four random digits are added. gaming-0042. two groups can share a title; the digits tell them apart.

  • a badge. 1 to 12 letters. shown in capitals.
  • a colour.
  • an about line. up to 200.
  • a picture and a banner, if you like.

you are in it from the start. you are its owner.

owners and roles

the maker is an owner. an owner has every power. an owner can name another owner, and take it back. nobody else can. not a site admin.

a group keeps one owner while anyone else is in it. the last owner cannot leave until they name another. the last person out deletes the group.

a role: a name, a colour, and any of five powers.

  • appearance. the about line. public or private. the member order.
  • roles. make roles and hand them out. never owner.
  • channels. add, rename, order, delete.
  • kick.
  • ban. a ban stops a join until it is lifted.

joining and leaving are your own choice. no invitations. no approval.

a server

/groups is where you find them: yours, and ones to discover. and where you make one, while you are under the cap.

/groups/<name> is the room. a banner. the picture. the channel list. the chat. the roster on the right, with a search.

  1. a server starts with one channel, general. it stays.
  2. add another, rename it, drag the order, delete it. the channels power.
  3. the room loads once. after that it is pushed to. a new channel, a new line, a rename: it arrives.
  4. mute a channel and the bell hears nothing from it.
  5. look at a channel and it is read, on every device of yours.

home lists servers under recently offline. a chat line in a channel is a block, not a stream's line.
