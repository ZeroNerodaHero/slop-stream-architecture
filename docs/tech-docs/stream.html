a stream

one upload in. many copies out. from go live to the end.

go live

  1. you open /live. your camera shows in the preview.
  2. you pick your sources. or not. the camera alone is fine.
  3. you press start. you stream as your account, or as the nick in the corner.
  4. the browser sets up with video over /ws. the packets go to udp 7882.
  5. video has your stream. a live fact goes out. your card appears on home.
  6. watchers come. video copy your stream to each one.

you send one copy. video send one copy to each watcher. your upload never grows with the crowd.

camera only works on https. the warning you click through is enough.

sources and layers

beside the preview, your sources. the camera. a screen or a window. pictures and gifs you put up. your avatar. add one with the +, before start or while live.

each source is a layer over the preview. drag it. pull a corner. order them. hide one. what you see is what watchers see.

presets: screen + face, side by side, and the rest. or save your own.

every watcher's player draws the same frame from the scene. nothing is mixed on the server. sharp text at the same 3 Mbps.

mic only

× on the camera turns it off. your avatar takes its place. it moves with your voice. every player draws it from your name and the sound. nothing extra is sent.

it stays that way next visit, until you add the camera back.

under the preview: the sound. a fader and a meter for the mic. a mute. what everyone hears.

while live

  • you get a watch link to copy. paste it in a chat and it unfurls: your name, live or not, your status, the picture.
  • leave the page and the stream keeps going. it sits in a corner of every other page. your chat with it. × on the corner is stop.
  • video make a jpeg of your stream every 5 s. home shows it. /thumb/<name>.jpg. no cdn. no bucket.
  • N watching: video count connected watchers. one per ip. capped per ip. hard to spam.

right now: watching. the number climbs as they arrive.

the end

three things can end a stream:

  1. you press stop.
  2. video notices your packets stopped. the publisher is gone.
  3. the sweeper finds a session nobody closed.

each one closes the block in the log. the block says who closed it. an ended fact goes out. home drops your card.

the calendar

the calendar tab on the watch page. when they stream. one block per session, from the log. a week strip with a line for now.

not built yet. plan only. the totals on the profile tab. a schedule you set.

fake streams

for styling without a camera. in .env:

DUMMY_STREAMS=3
DUMMY_PASSWORD=anyword

then ./scripts/deploy.sh dummy. home fills with dummy-mango, dummy-pixel, dummy-disco. three gifs each, bouncing like the dvd logo. a clock. a quiet tone. about a tenth of a core each.

the password is what lets the dummy box use dummy- names. nobody else can. set it back to 0 and run it again to turn them off.

not built yet. plan only. more than one video size (720, 360 copies made on the box). hold the mouse on a card and it plays. up to 4 people on the watch page coming on with a mic.
