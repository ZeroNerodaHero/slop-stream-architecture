people

who you are. who is here. who you follow. what reaches you.

a name

nobody has to log in. the name box in the corner panel is you. it lives in your browser. it is your chat nick and your go live name. any name nobody owns is free.

sign up, and the name is yours. nobody else can go live as it. or chat as it. or change its page. a ✓ next to a name means a login is behind it.

a name used without an account, then claimed: the status, the page and the pictures stay.

database

the browser sends the password as it is. https hides the trip. the api scramble it. argon2id is the scrambler. it is slow on purpose. only the scramble is kept. the real password is never kept.

why not scramble in the browser. because then the scramble is the password. a thief with the database could log in with it. nothing gained.

an id under the name

every name has an id that never changes. follows, groups, pictures and messages point at the id. not the name.

so you can change your name. the profile tab. once a month. with your password. not while live. everything follows.

the old name is held 30 days. old links still find you.

presence

the square on the corner of your picture. the word next to your name on your page.

  • online. a tab of the site is open.
  • streaming. you are live.
  • do not disturb. you picked it.
  • offline. you picked appear offline, or closed the last tab.

redis

one wire per browser, shared by all its tabs. it says which names it watches. a fact about one of them comes down the wire. nothing on a page asks again and again.

the wire drops: back in 3 s. it replays the last 10 s. the last tab closes: offline at once.

follows

you follow a name. one row: you, them. they follow you back: you are friends.

the side column shows who you follow. friends apart from the rest. the ones streaming first. a search box to find more.

the watch page has the button, and followers.
