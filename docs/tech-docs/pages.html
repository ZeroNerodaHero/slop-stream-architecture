the map

urlwhat

/groupsyour groups, and ones to discover. make one.

every page but one is the same html, drawn by the browser. /watch/<name> comes from the api with that name's title and preview tags filled in. so a shared link gets a card.

home

every live stream, with a picture of it a few seconds old. click one to watch.

under them, everyone else. a rectangle each: their picture, the square on its corner, the name, the word. online first.

the page asks the home box once. then it listens on the wire. a stream starting appears without a reload.

go live

your camera in the preview. your sources beside it. start at the foot of the sources panel. see a stream.

a watch link to copy. your status and your picture are set on the profile tab, not here.

watch

the player. their name, the square, the word, their status line under the name. the chat beside it. N watching. the follow button and N followers.

three tabs under the player:

  • about. their about me, a few paragraphs. then a page of their own, in blocks: words, pictures, a clip, a music player, links. their own css over it, cleaned first. no html. media from this site only. an owner writes it in place.
  • calendar. when they stream. one block per session.
  • socials. where else to find them. links as icons. tiktok, youtube, twitch, discord, x, a globe for anything else. a row or a grid. twenty at most.

they are not live: the page is their profile. the picture big. the word. the tabs. the chat. a streamer can set an offline picture where the player would be, and a line like back tuesday at 8. the camera appears the moment they start.

the side column

the left side. or the right, from settings. closed, it is pictures and squares, you at the bottom. the page uses the rest.

hover it, or tab into it. it widens over the page. the page stays where it is.

  • who you follow. friends apart. the ones streaming first. the word beside each name.
  • the search box. the site's one. find people, follow them.
  • a chat window for a friend. a tab per friend.
  • your picture at the bottom is your own page. the gear at the end of your line is settings.

the title row

sticks to the top while the page scrolls. go live at the right end. before it, the bell: your inbox, with how many are new.

settings

the gear opens the user modal. eight tabs in two groups.

youthis browser
profile: picture, status, presence, offline picture, the name change.
groups: the ones you are in.
security: password, log out everywhere.
notifications: the four switches. 		appearance: the theme, the text size.
chat: where the panel sits.
sidebar: left or right.
advanced: the messages inspector.

the first group is saved with your account. the second is this browser only.

the corner

you are live and you leave the page. the stream keeps going in a corner of every other page. your chat with it. the picture in picture button on the preview is that same move. × is stop.

the look

dark. hard edges. a box is a 1px stroke. no shadows anywhere. a hover is a colour or a border change. a small action is words, not a button.
