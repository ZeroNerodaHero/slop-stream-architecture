# docs

- `overview.md` — the short one. what this is, how to deploy, test, and work on it. read this first.
- `idea.md` — the product, not a build. open groups where people show their face and talk, a person's own stream they can decorate, and one person sharing audio for everyone else to hear. `overview.md` is what runs.
- `spec.md` — the long one. every port, route, env, timeout and rule, for a person or an llm building on it.
- `chat.md` — the text chat: why redis streams and a websocket, the protocol, the limits, the panel.
- `architecture.md` — background from before the build. the big picture of calls vs broadcasts, not the repo layout.
- `architecture-picture.md` — the repo as pictures. what runs, what talks to what, what builds into what. no new rules, just diagrams of what is already here.
- `accounts.md` — username + password, optional: argon2id on the api, hashed login tokens in a cookie, what an account protects, what a breach gets.
- `groups.md` — a login joins a group and its chat lines wear the group's badge, discord style. `bot` is built in and automatic (a socket says `bot: true`), and the chat panel can hide the bots' lines.
- `ids.md` — built. a uuid on every `streamers` row that never changes; logins, memberships, groups and the bucket keys point at it instead of the name, so a login can change its name (`POST /api/name`, the profile tab: once a month, with its password, not while live) and keep everything. the old name held 30 days with links that follow. the migration, proven on a copy first and run at boot on the real database, with a way back.
- `follows.md` — built, the first half. a login follows a name: one edge table in postgres keyed on the ids of `ids.md` (why not a graph database, redis sets or a counter column), the box over the corner panel (who you follow, streaming first, and a search box to find more), `N followers` and the button on the watch page. still a plan: "alice went live" pushed to every tab and device, gated by `do not disturb`; followed streams first on home; the modal tab.
- `posts.md` — a plan, not a build. a login writes a short post, with one picture or without, and a reply is a post pointing at another: one table in postgres (why a bigint id, why no counters, why a like is the one thing that wants a second table), the feed as a read over `follows` and not a timeline per follower, and the post itself as one fact on the `events` stream so every follower's tab has it the moment it lands.
- `kubernetes.md` — a plan, not a build. what k8s would buy here, the video routing problem, the code to change first, the steps.
- `debugging.md` — how you find out something broke. the health answer, the pages reporting their own failures to `/api/errors`, the separate debug database, `/admin`, and the monitor box that polls every box.
- `metrics.md` — a plan, not a build. cpu, memory, disk and network per container and per box, in our own code: an `api agent` on every box, the monitor keeping and drawing it, alerts on the lines that matter. the questions to settle first.
- `status.md` — presence, built: `offline`, `online`, `do not disturb`, `streaming` next to a name. a gateway websocket in a container of its own (`events`), one per browser in a shared worker, open on every page; sorted sets in redis for "here", one stream for the facts, one column for the choice, `streaming` from what the api already knows. plus the messages inspector: the frames the browser gets, the last 1000 in a ring, in a panel over the page. every container's change, the tests, the questions still open.
- `transition.md` — a plan, not a build. the move from polling to the socket in `status.md`: why the first draft said no, every http loop the pages run today and which ones become pushes, what stays http and why, old pages against a new server during each step, the deploy order with its proof and its way back, the numbers to check before it goes out.
- `hover-preview.md` — a plan, not a build. hold the mouse on a card and it plays the stream; click and the page uses the connection the card made, so the first frame is instant. where a click's time goes today, why hover and not mousedown, the one refactor in `watch-atoms.ts`. once `bitrates.md` exists the card joins the 360 rung and the click steps that same peer up.
- `viewers.md` — built. `N watching`: video counts connected watcher peers, one per ip, capped per ip. why it is hard to spam, and what cloudflare would and would not add.
- `bitrates.md` — a plan, not a build. the streamer keeps sending one 1080p vp8. the ingest box makes the 720 and 360 copies, and a watcher gets one of those sizes.
- `audio.md` — a plan, not a build. go live with a mic and no camera, and while live open up to 4 spots so people on the watch page come on with a mic, or a mic and a camera.
- `screen.md` — built. the screen share, aimed at a tab of some other site: sharp text at the same 1080p30 vp8 and 3 Mbps, and the camera laid over the screen where the streamer drags it on the go live page's preview, which every watcher's player draws the same from a few numbers in the slots frame (or beside it as a tile). the tab's sound stays in `audio.md`. obs stays later.
- `calendar.md` — a plan, not a build. a calendar on a person's page of when they stream: one block per session, from the `sessions` table that already has them. the three things that close a block when a connection drops (the page's stop, video noticing the publisher gone, the sweeper), the one column that makes a swept block end where the stream did, a range on the sessions route, the 28-day strip and the `usually` line.
