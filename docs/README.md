# docs

the short start for a person who just cloned the repo is the root `README.md`. this folder is the long docs. read `overview.md` first. a doc that says "a plan, not a build" describes something not in the repo; a plan is not stale.

the code carries no comments (`.cursor/rules/no-comments.mdc`). where a file's reasons used to be written in it, they are in the source notes at the end of this list.

the database is one file, `api/schema.sql`, applied by the api at boot to an empty database (`api/src/schema.rs`). it is the baseline as of 2026-11-19 (the old migrations folded into it); a change to the tables from here is a new `api/migrations/NNNN-what.sql`, applied at the api's next boot, no reset (`api/migrations/README.md`). a `migrate-<n>` left in a topic file (the history block in `groups.md`) is the order things were built in, not a file.

## the product and the whole

- `overview.md` — the short one. what this is, how to deploy, test, and work on it. read this first.
- `idea.md` — the product, not a build. open groups where people show their face and talk, a person's own stream they can decorate, and one person sharing audio for everyone else to hear. `overview.md` is what runs.
- `spec.md` — the long one. every port, route, env, timeout and rule, for a person or an llm building on it.
- `chat.md` — the text chat: why redis streams and a websocket, the protocol, the limits, the panel.
- `rt.md` — built, server side. one always-on websocket per browser for chat and events topics (`/rt` in the chat process): full-set `sub` frames with a rising `rev`, the caps, `say` and `react` with an ack, ticket auth for a login without a reconnect.
- `chats.md` — the map of the three chats. a stream's room, a group's channel, and a direct message: where each one is, who may write, what is kept. the rules stay in `chat.md`, `groups.md` and `dms.md`.
- `architecture.md` — background from before the build. the big picture of calls vs broadcasts, not the repo layout.
- `architecture-picture.md` — the repo as pictures. what runs, what talks to what, what builds into what. no new rules, just diagrams of what is already here.
- `accounts.md` — username + password, optional: argon2id on the api, hashed login tokens in a cookie, what an account protects, what a breach gets.
- `destinations.md` — twitch, kick and custom rtmp: connecting accounts, sealed stream keys, the routes, and the phase 0 relay test.
- `multichat.md` — twitch and kick chat read into slop chat: the kick webhook, the twitch eventsub socket, who sees it, and the picker.

## topics

- `groups.md` — a login joins a group and its chat lines wear the group's badge, discord style. `bot` is built in and automatic (a socket says `bot: true`), and the chat panel can hide the bots' lines. a group is also a server: a picture, a banner, an owner, custom roles, and a room whose chat is blocks rather than the stream's lines. home lists them under recently offline, `/groups` is where a login makes one.
- `ids.md` — built. a uuid on every `streamers` row that never changes; logins, memberships, groups and the bucket keys point at it instead of the name, so a login can change its name (`POST /api/name`, the profile tab: once a month, with its password, not while live) and keep everything. the old name held 30 days with links that follow. the migration, proven on a copy first and run at boot on the real database, with a way back.
- `follows.md` — built, the first half. a login follows a name: one edge table in postgres keyed on the ids of `ids.md` (why not a graph database, redis sets or a counter column), the sidebar beside the page (who you follow, streaming first, friends who follow back, the site's search box), `N followers` and the button on the watch page, the followed streams as a part of home, and "alice went live" as a row in every follower's inbox (`notifications.md`). still a plan: the toast, the modal tab with the followers list.
- `posts.md` — a plan, not a build. a login writes a short post, with one picture or without, and a reply is a post pointing at another: one table in postgres (why a bigint id, why no counters, why a like is the one thing that wants a second table), the feed as a read over `follows` and not a timeline per follower, and the post itself as one fact on the `events` stream so every follower's tab has it the moment it lands.
- `dms.md` — built. direct messages between friends (a login and a name that follow each other): one table of lines keyed on the ids of `ids.md`, http to write and to read a page, the line pushed to every device of both sides through the events gateway, a read mark per thread so the count of what is new holds across reloads and devices. the window: a tab per friend, the grid to pick one, the counts on the sidebar.
- `notifications.md` — built. a login's inbox: the last 30 things that happened to them (someone they follow went live, a new follower, a friend's line), one table keyed on the ids of `ids.md`, clipped to 30 on every write, one `notify` fact per thing naming every login it reached, pushed to each one's own sockets. the bell at the right end of the title row with how many are new and the list under it; which kinds are kept as three switches on a `notifications` tab of the user modal, saved with the account.
- `kubernetes.md` — a plan, not a build. what k8s would buy here, the video routing problem, the code to change first, the steps.
- `debugging.md` — how you find out something broke. the health answer, the pages reporting their own failures to `/api/errors`, the separate debug database, `/admin`, and the monitor (`api monitor`, its page and compose file in `monitor/`). the monitor box itself, the watcher bot and grafana are not built.
- `metrics.md` — cpu, memory, disk and network per container and per box, in our own code: `api agent` on every box, the monitor keeping an hour, a day and a week and drawing it at https://stats.nodentity.xyz behind basic auth. setting its password, adding a vm, who can reach what. alerts on the numbers are still a plan.
- `status.md` — presence, built: `offline`, `online`, `do not disturb`, `streaming` next to a name. a gateway websocket in a container of its own (`events`), one per browser in a shared worker, open on every page; sorted sets in redis for "here", one stream for the facts, one column for the choice, `streaming` from what the api already knows. plus the messages inspector: the frames the browser gets, the last 1000 in a ring, in a panel over the page. every container's change, the tests, the questions still open.
- `transition.md` — history. the move from polling to the socket in `status.md`, which has happened: why the first draft said no, every http loop the pages ran and which became pushes, what stays http and why, the deploy order with its proof and its way back.
- `hover-preview.md` — half built. hold the mouse on a home card and it plays the stream, muted, on a preview peer of its own (`app/home/hover.ts`, `infra/preview.ts`). still a plan: the click taking over the card's connection so the first frame is instant, the refactor in `watch-atoms.ts` that needs.
- `viewers.md` — built. `N watching`: video counts connected watcher peers, one per ip, capped per ip. why it is hard to spam, and what cloudflare would and would not add.
- `bitrates.md` — a plan, not a build. the streamer keeps sending one 1080p vp8. the ingest box makes the 720 and 360 copies, and a watcher gets one of those sizes.
- `audio.md` — a plan, partly built: the mix in the page (the mic into one opus, with a fader, a mute and a meter, the audio section always offered) is `screen.md`'s mixer, and go live with a mic and no camera is `screen.md`'s mic only. still a plan: home's card for a mic-only stream, a tab's sound, and while live open up to 4 spots so people on the watch page come on with a mic, or a mic and a camera.
- `screen.md` — built. the screen share and the frame around it, the obs idea as numbers: sources in a sidebar (the camera, screens and windows, pictures and gifs put up at the api, the avatar), layers dragged and resized over the go live page's preview, a mixer for the mic, and mic only (the camera off, the mic alone, the streamer's picture moving with their voice for a face, drawn by every player from the name and the sound, nothing sent); every watcher's player draws the same frame from the scene in the slots frame, nothing composited. sharp text at the same 1080p30 vp8 and 3 Mbps. obs itself stays later.
- `vtuber.md` — partly built. a face cam without the face: a streamer picks a 3d model (one of the site's demo models, or a file of their own that stays in their browser: a `.vrm`, or a `.glb` or an `.fbx` out of another tool), and their browser reads their face from the camera, moves the model, draws it in a worker, and sends the drawing in the camera's slot. video and the watchers did not change, and the camera's own picture never leaves. the demo models (`random-media/vrms`, `scripts/models.sh`, `GET /api/models`), the file and how it is kept in the browser (indexeddb: a model is downloaded once), other kinds of file (a glb or an fbx says nothing of where its head is, so it is a model by the names of its bones and its shapes, made a vrm in memory), the face reader and why its version is pinned, the face (which of a model's own shapes the reader moves: the mouth and the blinks, the brows and a smile on a vroid model, all 52 on a perfect sync one), the rigging (a panel under the preview where the streamer, and nobody else, sees the reader's marks on their own camera's picture and the numbers they become), the bar while a model loads, the hidden tab and the worker that gets past it, the pages' ids, the test, what was run and what was not. then what is still a plan: the rest of a face, and the later way that cuts the model out over the screen (numbers on the socket, drawn by every watcher).
- `calendar.md` — built, the first half: the `calendar` tab on the watch page, when they stream: one block per session, from the `sessions` table that already had them. the three things that close a block when a connection drops (the page's stop, video noticing the publisher gone, the sweeper), the columns that make a swept block end where the stream did and say who closed it, the range on the sessions route, the week strip and its line. still a plan: the totals on the profile tab, the schedule.
- `about.md` — built: the `about` tab under the player, a page of the person's own on a canvas: blocks of fixed kinds (text, a picture, a clip, a sound, lines and boxes) with a place, a size and a few checked look fields, the files uploaded and moderated, the text a cleaned fragment of a few html tags. no scripts and no css of their own. a sample until the name saves a page. the older `about` column (the about me) is still on the reads.
- `socials.md` — built. the `socials` tab under the player, after `about` and `calendar`: where else to find them, their links as icons (tiktok, youtube, twitch, discord, x and the rest, a globe for any other site), in a row or a grid, at a size they pick. one jsonb document on `streamers`, replaced whole through the bio's gate, checked at the door (http(s) with a host and nothing else, a kind off the list refused, twenty at most) and again by the page before it draws; the editor on the tab itself, under the strip on the owner's own page, with one save, the strip following the draft as it is typed. with it the corner panel changed: the picture is your own page, the gear moved from the title row to the end of your line, the `following` button went.
- `analytics.md` — built. a streamer's own stats on the **stats** tab of the user modal: viewers over time, peak and average, time live, watch time, unique viewers, follows while live, chat lines. logins only, nobody else's.
- `voice.md` — built, off on the live box. voice channels in a group: who is in each on the group page's right, the call where the chat is, cameras and screens in it, `video voice` as the server.
- `keyframes.md` — built. how video gets a joining watcher its first picture: keyframe requests to the publisher, not a cache of the last keyframe.
- `vp9.md` — built. the camera in vp9 simulcast when the browser encodes it well, a small vp8 copy for watchers that cannot decode vp9, how each watcher's codec is picked from its own offer, the numbers.
- `jannies.md` — who may clean a stream's chat, and only its chat. `api/src/janny.rs`.
- `test-streamers.md` — built, backend only. the dummy box's fake streams as `streamers` rows flagged `is_test` (migration `0017-test-streamers.sql`), the dummy secret as an `X-Dummy-Password` header, the narrowed rate-limit skip, and `HIDE_TEST_STREAMERS`, which leaves them out of every list.
- `rate-limits.md` — every route's limit (`api/src/ratelimit.rs`, `video/src/limits.rs`), the `429` with `Retry-After`, the numbers.
- `image-moderation.md` — every uploaded picture checked by openai's moderation endpoint before the api keeps it (`api/src/moderate.rs`, migration `0015-image-moderation.sql`), the adult lock on groups, the follow-ups not built.
- `chat-audit.md` — a dated audit (2026-11-19): why picture in picture showed the wrong chat, every chat surface, the bugs and the fixes. a record; the names in it are that day's.
