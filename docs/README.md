# docs

- `overview.md` — the short one. what this is, how to deploy, test, and work on it. read this first.
- `idea.md` — the product, not a build. open groups where people show their face and talk, a person's own stream they can decorate, and one person sharing audio for everyone else to hear. `overview.md` is what runs.
- `spec.md` — the long one. every port, route, env, timeout and rule, for a person or an llm building on it.
- `chat.md` — the text chat: why redis streams and a websocket, the protocol, the limits, the panel.
- `chats.md` — the map of the three chats. a stream's room, a group's channel, and a direct message: where each one is, who may write, what is kept. the rules stay in `chat.md`, `groups.md` and `dms.md`.
- `architecture.md` — background from before the build. the big picture of calls vs broadcasts, not the repo layout.
- `architecture-picture.md` — the repo as pictures. what runs, what talks to what, what builds into what. no new rules, just diagrams of what is already here.
- `accounts.md` — username + password, optional: argon2id on the api, hashed login tokens in a cookie, what an account protects, what a breach gets.
- `groups.md` — a login joins a group and its chat lines wear the group's badge, discord style. `bot` is built in and automatic (a socket says `bot: true`), and the chat panel can hide the bots' lines. a group is also a server: a picture, a banner, an owner, custom roles, and a room whose chat is blocks rather than the stream's lines. home lists them under recently offline, `/groups` is where a login makes one.
- `ids.md` — built. a uuid on every `streamers` row that never changes; logins, memberships, groups and the bucket keys point at it instead of the name, so a login can change its name (`POST /api/name`, the profile tab: once a month, with its password, not while live) and keep everything. the old name held 30 days with links that follow. the migration, proven on a copy first and run at boot on the real database, with a way back.
- `follows.md` — built, the first half. a login follows a name: one edge table in postgres keyed on the ids of `ids.md` (why not a graph database, redis sets or a counter column), the box over the corner panel (who you follow, streaming first, and a search box to find more), `N followers` and the button on the watch page. still a plan: "alice went live" pushed to every tab and device, gated by `do not disturb`; followed streams first on home; the modal tab.
- `posts.md` — a plan, not a build. a login writes a short post, with one picture or without, and a reply is a post pointing at another: one table in postgres (why a bigint id, why no counters, why a like is the one thing that wants a second table), the feed as a read over `follows` and not a timeline per follower, and the post itself as one fact on the `events` stream so every follower's tab has it the moment it lands.
- `dms.md` — built. direct messages between friends (a login and a name that follow each other): one table of lines keyed on the ids of `ids.md`, http to write and to read a page, the line pushed to every device of both sides through the events gateway, a read mark per thread so the count of what is new holds across reloads and devices. the window: a tab per friend, the grid to pick one, the counts on the sidebar.
- `notifications.md` — built. a login's inbox: the last 30 things that happened to them (someone they follow went live, a new follower, a friend's line), one table keyed on the ids of `ids.md`, clipped to 30 on every write, one `notify` fact per thing naming every login it reached, pushed to each one's own sockets. the bell at the right end of the title row with how many are new and the list under it; which kinds are kept as three switches on a `notifications` tab of the user modal, saved with the account.
- `kubernetes.md` — a plan, not a build. what k8s would buy here, the video routing problem, the code to change first, the steps.
- `debugging.md` — how you find out something broke. the health answer, the pages reporting their own failures to `/api/errors`, the separate debug database, `/admin`, and the monitor box that polls every box.
- `metrics.md` — a plan, not a build. cpu, memory, disk and network per container and per box, in our own code: an `api agent` on every box, the monitor keeping and drawing it, alerts on the lines that matter. the questions to settle first.
- `status.md` — presence, built: `offline`, `online`, `do not disturb`, `streaming` next to a name. a gateway websocket in a container of its own (`events`), one per browser in a shared worker, open on every page; sorted sets in redis for "here", one stream for the facts, one column for the choice, `streaming` from what the api already knows. plus the messages inspector: the frames the browser gets, the last 1000 in a ring, in a panel over the page. every container's change, the tests, the questions still open.
- `transition.md` — a plan, not a build. the move from polling to the socket in `status.md`: why the first draft said no, every http loop the pages run today and which ones become pushes, what stays http and why, old pages against a new server during each step, the deploy order with its proof and its way back, the numbers to check before it goes out.
- `hover-preview.md` — a plan, not a build. hold the mouse on a card and it plays the stream; click and the page uses the connection the card made, so the first frame is instant. where a click's time goes today, why hover and not mousedown, the one refactor in `watch-atoms.ts`. once `bitrates.md` exists the card joins the 360 rung and the click steps that same peer up.
- `viewers.md` — built. `N watching`: video counts connected watcher peers, one per ip, capped per ip. why it is hard to spam, and what cloudflare would and would not add.
- `bitrates.md` — a plan, not a build. the streamer keeps sending one 1080p vp8. the ingest box makes the 720 and 360 copies, and a watcher gets one of those sizes.
- `audio.md` — a plan, partly built: the mix in the page (the mic into one opus, with a fader, a mute and a meter, the audio section always offered) is `screen.md`'s mixer, and go live with a mic and no camera is `screen.md`'s mic only. still a plan: home's card for a mic-only stream, a tab's sound, and while live open up to 4 spots so people on the watch page come on with a mic, or a mic and a camera.
- `screen.md` — built. the screen share and the frame around it, the obs idea as numbers: sources in a sidebar (the camera, screens and windows, pictures and gifs put up at the api, the avatar), layers dragged and resized over the go live page's preview, presets by role, a mixer for the mic, and mic only (the camera off, the mic alone, the streamer's picture moving with their voice for a face, drawn by every player from the name and the sound, nothing sent); every watcher's player draws the same frame from the scene in the slots frame, nothing composited. sharp text at the same 1080p30 vp8 and 3 Mbps. obs itself stays later.
- `calendar.md` — built, the first half: the `calendar` tab on the watch page, when they stream: one block per session, from the `sessions` table that already had them. the three things that close a block when a connection drops (the page's stop, video noticing the publisher gone, the sweeper), the columns that make a swept block end where the stream did and say who closed it, the range on the sessions route, the 28-day strip and the `usually` line. still a plan: the totals on the profile tab, the schedule.
- `about.md` — built: the `about` tab under the player. first on it the about me, a few paragraphs of theirs (`about` on the row, through the bio's gate, on the read and the grant), written in place by the owner on the tab itself; the status (what was the bio) is the one line under the name. then the viewer's half of a page of the person's own in blocks (words, pictures, a clip, a music player, links) with css of their own over it, drawn from a sample until its editor exists. the rules that keep it a page of boxes: no html, media from this origin only, http(s) links, and css parsed by the browser, kept to style rules and `@media`, every selector scoped under the box, every declaration that loads or runs or leaves taken out, in a box that contains and clips. what hostile css becomes, and what is not built (the route, the editor, uploads).
- `socials.md` — built. the `socials` tab under the player, after `about` and `calendar`: where else to find them, their links as icons (tiktok, youtube, twitch, discord, x and the rest, a globe for any other site), in a row or a grid, at a size they pick. one jsonb document on `streamers`, replaced whole through the bio's gate, checked at the door (http(s) with a host and nothing else, a kind off the list refused, twenty at most) and again by the page before it draws; the editor on the tab itself, under the strip on the owner's own page, with one save, the strip following the draft as it is typed. with it the corner panel changed: the picture is your own page, the gear moved from the title row to the end of your line, the `following` button went.
